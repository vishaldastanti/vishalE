Our Story & Legacy

Pioneering Modern Apparel Manufacturing in Bihar

Founded with a vision to redefine ready-made garment manufacturing, Vishal Enterprises has grown into a high-capacity apparel production power in Vaishali, Bihar. We specialise in full-package OEM/ODM garment manufacturing—spanning initial design interpretation, pattern development, fabric sourcing, cutting, precision stitching, printing, embroidery, and dispatch.

Our facility serves B2B wholesale distributors, online private label brands, corporate organizations, and retail garment chains looking for premium fabric feel, consistent sizing, and dependable volume fulfillment.
